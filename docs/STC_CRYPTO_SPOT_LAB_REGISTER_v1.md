# STC Crypto Spot Trading Lab — Register Checkpoint

**Date:** 2026-09-30  
**Project Transition:** STC Competition Trading Engine → STC Crypto Spot Research & Paper Trading Lab

## 1. Decision Recorded

The primary project objective is transitioned from TradingView competition tracking to a research and testing system for cryptocurrency trading under:

**Spot Trading Only**

Excluded in this phase:
- Futures
- Leverage
- Short Trading

## 2. Main Objective

Build a testable cryptocurrency trading research system supporting:
- Historical Study
- Backtesting
- Walk Forward Testing
- Paper Trading
- Forward Performance Tracking

No real-money promotion is authorized by this checkpoint.

## 3. Research Philosophy

Do not apply one arbitrary strategy to every asset.

Each asset must be studied by:
- Price history
- Bull/Bear cycle behavior
- Volatility
- Liquidity
- Volume behavior
- Trend behavior
- Breakout behavior
- Pullback behavior

Strategy selection must follow measured asset behavior.

## 8. Important Constraint

The objective is not to select assets because they previously rose sharply.

Research must answer:
- What type of asset is it?
- When does it move?
- Which strategy fits it?
- When do we enter?
- When do we exit?
- When do we not trade?
